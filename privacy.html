Privacy policy for rclone-backup

rclone-backup is a personal tool used only by its author to back up their own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • What it accesses: only my own Google Drive account, using read-only access (the drive.readonly scope). It never changes or deletes anything in Google Drive.
  • Where data is stored: only on my own devices: my own computer and a storage drive I own at home.
  • Sharing: nothing is shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.
